About Us

London, United Kingdom

Digital Shamans is a game art outsourcing studio. Our team of game developers and freelance artists works alongside studios to raise the visual quality of their games and help meet production targets.

We develop concept art, environments and game-ready 3D assets, and help integrate and optimise them in-engine. Our work spans PC, console, mobile and VR.
