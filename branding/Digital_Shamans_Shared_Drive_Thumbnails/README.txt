Digital Shamans Shared Drive Thumbnails — v3

Use the 15 PNG files in Themes for Google Drive shared-drive custom themes.
Each file is 1280 x 144 pixels (80:9), with the complete illustration centered
inside the thumbnail area. The original blue-purple 3D style is retained.
Large embedded titles are removed; Google Drive supplies the drive names.

Apply a theme:
1. Extract this ZIP and upload the matching Themes PNG to Google Drive.
2. Open your shared drive (Manager access is required).
3. Click the drive name > Change theme > Create custom theme.
4. Choose the matching uploaded PNG.
5. Keep the complete wide strip in the crop selection; avoid zooming in.
6. Confirm the selection.

The preview models a 56-pixel circular center crop, matching the crop observed
in your supplied screenshot. The PNG dimensions and all 15 previews have been
checked locally. These files have not been applied to your live Google Drive.
Google Drive's presentation may differ between views or future UI versions.
